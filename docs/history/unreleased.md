<summary><strong>Now and Schedule</strong></summary>

- The Now profile card shows Kano's tagline and catchphrase in the board's
  Berry, and a row of counts (upcoming entries, recent posts, recent videos)
  that open Schedule, Posts, and Videos. On iPhone it is a compact card with
  a shorter banner, the name beside the avatar, and no tagline.
- The Now tab carries an Unofficial mark at the top of its bar, and its
  title is Kano's greeting, こんまほ〜.
- The Now tab names the last data update in its navigation subtitle; the
  freshness footer shows only the data's status.
- Tapping the Now profile banner, avatar, or spotlight cover opens it in the
  image viewer.
- Schedule's List mode names a labelled stream by its activity, such as
  Gameplay, in the stream color, instead of an unknown type.

<summary><strong>Platform and Build</strong></summary>

- The app reads the Kano Navi service at its new address, kano.fans, and
  About links the web board there.
- Settings has a Server screen (a pane on Mac) to read the board from the
  Japan Server or the Mainland China Server, or from another Kano Navi service
  added by name and HTTPS address. The app checks an address before adding it,
  each server keeps its own saved copy, and About links the selected server's
  web board.
- Resizing a window on Mac or iPad keeps showing each picture while it moves
  and decodes it once at the size the window stops at, instead of at every
  size on the way.
- The sidebar on Mac and iPad lists Journey, Works, LINE BLOG, Settings, and
  About directly, under Archive and App, in place of More; on Mac it lists the
  archives only. Switching an iPad between the sidebar and the tab bar keeps
  the open screen.
- On Mac, Settings… (⌘,) opens a Settings window with General and
  Notifications panes, and About Kano Navi in the app menu opens an About
  window.
- Settings, Notifications, and About show rounded groups in a centered column
  on wide iPad and Mac windows. About opens with the app icon, name, and
  version, and names the models it was built with in one line.
- About links the app's source code and its license (AGPL-3.0) next to the
  service's source code.
- The Journey toolbar shows its Timeline and Gallery switch as one control,
  without a second capsule behind it.
