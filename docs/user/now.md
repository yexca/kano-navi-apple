# Now

The **Now** tab answers "is she live, what is next, and when" without scrolling.

## Profile Card

The top card shows Kano's banner, avatar, tagline, display name, romanized
name, catchphrase, and links to her X accounts (by `@handle`), YouTube channel,
and bilibili space. A row of counts below shows how many entries are coming up,
how many posts arrived in the last few days, and how many videos came out in
the last seven days; tapping a count opens **Schedule**, **Posts**, or
**Videos**. Tapping the banner or the avatar opens it in the full-screen image
viewer.

## Spotlight and Live Streams

- **Spotlight ticket:** Displays the live stream when she is on air, otherwise
  the next scheduled entry with a countdown and platform links, or the latest
  video when nothing is scheduled. Tapping its cover opens it in the image
  viewer.
- **Bilibili live:** When she is live on bilibili outside the spotlight entry, a
  dedicated live card appears.

## Coming Up and Recently

**Coming Up** lists the next five entries across today and the next 13 Japan
days (excluding the entry already in the spotlight and sale entries shown in
**On Sale**), grouped under **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or the weekday and date.
Each ticket shows the Japan time (or **About …** / **Time TBA**), a relative
time phrase, the title, platform, type, tag, and members or sale marks. Live
entries highlight in red with their end time; cancelled entries are struck
through. Tapping any entry opens it on the **Schedule** tab.

When nothing is scheduled in the next two weeks, the section offers a calendar
subscription link and shows the two most recently ended entries under
**Recently**.

## On Sale

Merchandise orders, pre-orders, ticket sales, and lotteries currently on sale
or opening soon appear with their opening and closing Japan times and links to
the shop or announcement.

## Freshness Footer

The time the service last collected data appears under the **Now** title. A
one-line footer at the bottom reports the data's status and warns in orange
when the last collection failed or a source has not been read for a day.
Tapping the footer opens a sheet with per-source read times and when your
device last checked.
