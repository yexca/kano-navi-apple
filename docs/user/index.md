# User Guide

Kano Navi for iOS and macOS is an unofficial fan app for Kano Mahoro (鹿乃まほろ)
on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It reads the public
[Kano Navi](https://kano.yexca.net/) status board and brings her upcoming
schedule, recent posts and videos, discography, journey since 2010, and LINE
BLOG archive into one native app.

These pages describe what you see and can do in the app:

- [Now](now.md): what is live or up next, the profile card, Coming Up, On Sale,
  and freshness status.
- [Schedule](schedule.md): List, Compact, Stacked, and Details month modes,
  entry details, and calendar subscriptions.
- [Posts](posts.md): the post timeline, account filter, month jump, and
  translations.
- [Videos](videos.md): On Air streams, recent videos, kind and platform filters,
  and month jump.
- [Journey](journey.md): milestones since 2010 and the visual archive.
- [Works](works.md): own releases, featured appearances, and uploaded songs.
- [LINE BLOG archive](lineblog.md): her 2015–2019 blog posts, photos, videos,
  and archived comments.
- [Widgets and notifications](widgets-and-notifications.md): Home Screen and
  Lock Screen widgets and on-device reminders.

## Navigation and Settings

The app has five tabs (**Now**, **Schedule**, **Posts**, **Videos**, and
**More**) in a tab bar on iPhone and on iPad. On Mac, and on iPad when you turn
the tab bar into a sidebar, the sidebar lists More's screens directly instead:
**Journey**, **Works**, and **LINE BLOG** under **Archive**, and **Settings**
and **About** under **App**.

- **Languages:** The interface supports English, Japanese, and Simplified
  Chinese through your system's per-app language setting (Language & Region on
  Mac). A separate **Content Language** setting under **Settings** chooses the
  language of post text, video titles, schedule titles, and archive
  descriptions (**Same as app**, English, 日本語, or 简体中文).
- **Appearance:** Light and dark appearances follow your system setting.
- **Japan time:** Event dates and times follow Japan Standard Time (JST).
- **Offline and cached data:** Each screen opens immediately with the copy last
  saved on your device and updates in the background. When the service cannot be
  reached, the saved copy remains visible beside a one-line notice and a **Try
  Again** button.
- **About:** **About** notes that the app is an unofficial fan project, links
  the Kano Navi web board and source repository, lists the AI models used to
  build the app, and shows the app version.

## Freshness and Official Sources

Kano Navi shows what the service last collected from X, YouTube, bilibili, and
the official shop; it is not a live feed. Schedule entries are marked
**Auto-detected** when read automatically or **Confirmed** when checked by the
board's maintainer. Every post, video, event, and archive item links to its
original public page, and official announcements always take precedence.
