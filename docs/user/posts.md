# Posts

The **Posts** tab presents a unified reverse-chronological timeline of posts
collected by the board, grouped by Japan month.

## Reading Posts

Each post card shows the account's avatar and handle (or platform name on
bilibili), the Japan timestamp, and a link to the original post on X or
bilibili. Promoted posts from related accounts carry an **Agency Post** chip.

- **Translations:** Post text follows your **Content Language** setting.
  Translated posts display a small origin mark and can be switched between the
  translation and the original text in place.
- **Media and quotes:** Attached photos (up to four thumbnails, with **+N** for
  more) open in the full-screen image viewer. Quoted posts appear nested inside
  the card, including deleted or withheld quote notices that keep the link to
  the original post.

## Filtering and Jumping

- **Account filter:** The leading menu filters the timeline to **All** or a
  specific X or bilibili account, showing post counts for each option.
- **Month picker:** The trailing calendar button opens a grid of years and
  months with post counts so you can jump directly to any month or back to the
  newest posts.
