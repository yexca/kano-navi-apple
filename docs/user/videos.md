# Videos

The **Videos** tab presents dated videos and live streams, grouped under **On
Air** (for active live or started streams) and Japan months.

## Video Cards

Each card displays a 16:9 cover with status or duration overlays (**Live**,
**Started**, **Upcoming**, **Premiere**, **Cancelled**, **Shorts**, or
members/private/removed availability), a platform mark, the title in your
chosen content language, and a line with the form, tag, and Japan date or time.

Tapping a video opens its watch page (prioritizing the selected platform when a
platform filter is active). Long-pressing or opening the context menu previews
the cover and lets you open alternate platforms, switch between translated and
original titles, copy the link, or share it.

## Filtering and Jumping

- **Kind filter:** Narrow to **All**, **Streams**, **Videos**, or **Shorts**.
- **Platform filter:** Narrow to **All**, **YouTube**, or **Bilibili**.
- **Month picker:** Jump to any month in the video index or return to **Newest**.
