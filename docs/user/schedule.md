# Schedule

The **Schedule** tab displays Monday-first months of Japan dates.

## Viewing Modes

The toolbar provides **Today** on the leading side and the mode menu and
calendar subscription menu on the trailing side. Four modes are available:

- **List (default):** Pins the active month above the selected day's entries.
  Under each day with entries, a segmented capsule shows up to three tag
  colors. Dragging the month vertically moves smoothly to the previous or next
  month; tapping a day selects it.
- **Compact:** Scrolls months vertically with colored dots under each day.
- **Stacked:** Scrolls months vertically with colored bars in taller cells.
- **Details:** Scrolls months vertically with inline titles on tinted pills.

Tapping any day in **Compact**, **Stacked**, or **Details** switches to **List**
for that day. At accessibility text sizes, **Stacked** and **Details** simplify
to dots so layouts remain legible.

## Entry Details

Each entry shows its tag color, title, status (**Live**, **On Sale**, **Opens**,
**Closes**, **Pending**, **Cancellation Suspected**, or **Cancelled**), type and
activity (such as **Gameplay**), and Japan start/end time (or **All Day**).
Tapping an entry opens a detail sheet with its full title, Japan time,
**Auto-detected** or **Confirmed** label, sale window, platform links, and a
link to the original announcement.

## Calendar Subscription

The calendar subscription menu lets you subscribe in your Calendar app
(`webcal://`) or share the HTTPS calendar feed link in the app's interface
language.
