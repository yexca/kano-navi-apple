# Works

The **Works** section under **More** collects Kano's discography and song
uploads.

## Entry Screen

The entry screen shows the year span, links to **Own Releases**, **Featured On**,
and **Uploads** with their counts, plus two highlights:

- **Latest Uploads:** The four newest uploads with covers and direct watch
  links.
- **On This Day:** Releases, participation works, and uploads whose Japan month
  and day match today in past years.

## Sections

- **Own Releases:** A cover grid (rows at accessibility text sizes) grouped by
  year, with a format filter (**All**, **Album**, **Single**, **EP**, **Doujin**,
  **Other**) and sort order switch. Opening a release shows all covers, catalog
  details, shared and edition-specific track lists, disc photos, store bonuses,
  and official streaming/download links with Hi-Res marks.
- **Featured On:** Participation works by year, highlighting the tracks Kano
  took part in and her credits.
- **Uploads:** Searchable list of uploaded songs joined with live cover and
  duration data, a kind filter, newer uploads above the collection cutoff, and
  watch links per platform (niconico, YouTube, members-only YouTube, bilibili).
