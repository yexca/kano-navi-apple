# Widgets and Notifications

## Widgets

Kano Navi includes **Upcoming** widgets for the Home Screen and desktop
(Small, Medium, Large, and Extra Large on iPad and Mac) and Lock Screen widgets
(Rectangular and Inline on iPhone and iPad).

- Small and Medium highlight the live or next entry with its countdown and
  Japan time; Medium adds up to three upcoming entries.
- Large and Extra Large add a seven-day Japan week strip with tag-colored dots
  and the week's schedule entries.
- Each widget lets you configure its **Content Language** independently in
  widget settings. Tapping an entry or day opens the corresponding event or date
  in the **Schedule** tab.

## Notifications and Reminders

Notifications run entirely on your device without an account or remote push
tokens. Permission is requested only when you enable **Allow Notifications**
under **More → Settings → Notifications**.

- **Immediate alerts:** Enable alerts for **Live Now**, **Posts** (own X,
  bilibili, and agency posts), **Videos** (works, Shorts, and optionally stream
  archives), **Schedule Updates** (filtered by category and an optional
  **Confirmed Entries Only** toggle), and **Members-only** content.
- **Timed reminders:** Schedule reminders for streams and events (at start, 10
  minutes before, or 30 minutes before) and merchandise sales (opening and 1, 6,
  or 24 hours before closing).
- Tapping a notification opens the related tab, entry, or settings screen
  directly.
