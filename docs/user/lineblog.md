# LINE BLOG Archive

The **LINE BLOG** section under **More** preserves Kano's 2015–2019 blog
articles recovered from web archives after LINE BLOG closed.

## Browsing and Reading

- **Article list:** Shows articles newest first under their Japan month, with a
  **Jump to Month** menu and an info sheet explaining how the archive and
  comments were preserved.
- **Reader:** Displays the article title, Japan timestamp, selectable text with
  in-line pictographs, pictures (tappable to open the image viewer with recovery
  notes and original links), quotes, link cards, embedded videos with in-place
  playback, older/newer article navigation, and source capture links.
- **Translations:** When **Content Language** is English or Simplified Chinese,
  machine translations appear below the title and each paragraph; a toolbar toggle
  lets you hide or show translations on the current article.
- **Comments:** Each article links to its archived visitor comments with
  original writer names, Japan timestamps, and ratings.
